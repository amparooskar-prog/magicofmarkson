The story behind the show

One trick.
A lifelong spark.

At seven, Markson’s dad introduced him to his first magic trick. That childhood fascination grew into a career performing for families, schools, shopping malls, cruise ships and corporate family days.

Becoming a father gave the work another meaning. The goal is simple: help every birthday child feel special, and give parents the chance to enjoy the moment with them.
